Description

No-bake Italian coffee-mascarpone dessert layered with ladyfingers.

Ingredients

  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 125 grams granulated sugar
  • 625 grams mascarpone cheese
  • 300 milliliters heavy cream
  • 2.5 tablespoons coffee liqueur (optional)
  • 375 milliliters strong brewed espresso, cooled
  • 2.5 tablespoons cocoa powder, for dusting
  • 30 ladyfinger cookies

Steps

  1. Whisk yolks and sugar: Whisk 5 large egg yolks and 125 grams granulated sugar
    together in a heatproof bowl until pale, then set over a pan of simmering water and whisk 8 minutes until thickened.
  2. Fold in mascarpone: Remove from heat and let cool slightly, then whisk in 625 grams mascarpone cheese until smooth.
  3. Whip the cream: In a separate bowl, whip 300 milliliters heavy cream to soft peaks, then fold into the mascarpone mixture.
  4. Mix the coffee soak: Combine 375 milliliters strong brewed espresso, cooled and 2.5 tablespoons coffee liqueur (optional) in a shallow dish.
  5. Dip and layer: Quickly dip each 30 ladyfinger cookies in the coffee mixture (don't soak) and arrange a layer in a dish. Spread half the mascarpone cream over top.
  6. Repeat and chill: Repeat with a second layer of dipped ladyfingers and remaining cream. Cover and chill at least 240 minutes, ideally overnight.
  7. Finish: Dust generously with 2.5 tablespoons cocoa powder, for dusting just before serving.
